03 · Welcome

A Legacy of
Warm Hospitality

Belvoir is a collection of fully furnished luxury hotel rooms, studio flats, apartments and residency. Located on Belvoir Avenue, 86 Wilkinson Road, one of the most prestigious and secure addresses in Freetown, our property offers the ideal setting for short and long stays. We also welcome yearly residency.

Backed by over 7 years of experience in Sierra Leone's real estate industry, we blend modern sophistication with the kind of warm, personalised service that makes every guest feel truly at home.

And we are still growing. A newly built wing of studio flats opens in 2026, adding up to twenty self-contained homes to the residence, each fully furnished and ready to move into.

09 · Good to Know

Questions,
Answered

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. The front desk is staffed 24 hours, so a late arrival is no problem — just let us know. Later check-out can often be arranged on request, subject to availability.
Breakfast is included with every hotel room. The studio flats and apartments are self-catering with full kitchens, and breakfast there is available on request. Our on-site restaurant serves local and continental food.
Belvoir Avenue, 86 Wilkinson Road, Freetown — one of the city's most sought-after addresses. Lumley and Aberdeen beaches are about 7 minutes away, the Lungi Airport water taxi 5 minutes, and the city centre 15 minutes. Gyms, restaurants, night clubs, supermarkets and sports clubs are all within walking distance.
Yes, pickup and drop-off are arranged on request. We can also help with Sea Coach water-taxi tickets from Lungi Airport. Contact us with your flight details and we will organise it before you arrive.
Rooms start at $70 per night, studio flats from $110 and apartments from $110, up to $170 for the two-bedroom. All rates are quoted per night in US dollars and include the room and the stated number of guests.
We accept bank card, Mobile Money and the Flot app. At checkout you choose your currency: pay the quoted amount in US dollars, or in Leones converted at the rate shown at the time of booking. Mobile Money settles in Leones only. You can pay in full or leave a 30% deposit and settle the balance at check-in.
Yes to both. Parking is free and private on site. The property has 24-hour electricity, so there is no interruption during your stay, and some apartments have their own electricity meter so you can manage and pay your own usage.
Free high-speed WiFi throughout the entire property, in every room and in the communal areas.
Yes. Extended stays start from $1,000 monthly and $14,000 annually, with tailor-made packages for business travellers, relocating guests and extended vacations. These are quoted separately — contact us and we will put something together.
Belvoir is a non-smoking property. There is a dedicated outdoor smoking area for guests who smoke.
If you paid in full, cancel more than 48 hours before your check-in date for a full refund. Inside 48 hours, or if you do not arrive, the first night is charged and the balance refunded. Deposits are non-refundable. Full details are on our booking terms page.
